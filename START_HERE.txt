DRAW WISE 5.4.0 — MAXIMUM INTELLIGENCE

NORMAL RUN
1. Double-click RUN_DRAW_WISE.bat
2. Choose a game
3. Choose 1, 3, 5, 10 or 20 lines
4. Choose an optimisation objective
5. Click GENERATE MY NUMBERS

WHAT V5.4 DOES
- Searches thousands of candidate lines.
- Optimises the whole portfolio, not each line in isolation.
- Controls pair/triple duplication and concentrated overlap.
- Uses a Randomness Gate to keep history secondary when results look uniform.
- Refines the portfolio with local/evolutionary search.
- Challenges the final portfolio against random portfolios on up to 100,000 synthetic fair draws.
- Reports exact portfolio top-prize odds separately from the optimisation score.

BUILD
- BUILD_DESKTOP_APP.bat creates a portable Windows desktop build.
- The separate DrawWise 5.4.0 Installer Builder creates DrawWise-Setup-5.4.0.exe.

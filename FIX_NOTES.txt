DrawWise 5.4.0 — Release Notes
==============================

Primary upgrade:
- Maximum Intelligence whole-portfolio optimisation replaces Smart Ensemble as
  the normal Smart Pick engine. Smart Ensemble remains available in Advanced Tools.

UI reliability:
- Advanced Tools is now in the permanent top navigation so it cannot be clipped.
- Generate My Numbers sits above the lower status cards and remains visible at the
  supported minimum window height.
- The result action buttons were compacted so Save is not clipped.

Mathematical boundary:
- The engine optimises portfolio coverage and match-threshold behaviour.
- It does not claim that a fair valid line has a different jackpot probability from
  any other fair valid line.

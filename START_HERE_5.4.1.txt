DRAWWISE 5.4.1 — MAXIMUM INTELLIGENCE + AI STRATEGY COPILOT

NORMAL USE
1. Run RUN_DRAW_WISE.bat (source) or open the installed DrawWise shortcut.
2. Choose a game.
3. Choose how many lines you want.
4. Leave the objective on Best overall portfolio unless you have a specific goal.
5. Click GENERATE MY NUMBERS.
6. Read the side-by-side Strategy Copilot review.

AI IS OPTIONAL
- Local strategy analysis works with no internet and no API key.
- For an independent OpenAI review, click Connect / change AI.
- Pasted API keys are session-only and are not saved to disk.
- The mathematical engine selects the portfolio; AI critiques it and may recommend a rerun/objective change.

ADVANCED TOOLS
Use the Advanced Tools button inside DrawWise. The installed shortcut always opens Maximum Intelligence first.

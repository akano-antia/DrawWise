# DrawWise 5.2 — Smart Pick Polish

- Fixed the V5.1 ticket-scroll defect where a newly generated multi-line portfolio could open with Line 02 or a later line at the top.
- Added a robust post-layout scroll reset so the **Recommended** card is always shown first after Generate, Regenerate and Pick Again.
- Changing the requested line count clears a mismatched old portfolio and returns the ticket area to the top.
- Changing games continues to clear the old portfolio and now also benefits from the same scroll reset.
- Made multi-line ticket cards more compact so more selections fit on screen.
- Simplified the everyday subtitle by removing per-line odds from the Smart Pick screen; detailed odds remain available in Advanced tools.
- Reduced unused vertical space in the empty state.
- Smart Ensemble scoring and lottery mathematics are unchanged from V5.1.
